A note for you

Baba,

One thing I really admire about you is how much the people in your life mean to you. I know you have your own way of showing it, but I think the connections you make become a part of you in ways you might not always notice. The people you spend time with, the memories you make with them, and the experiences you share all become pieces of your life that you get to carry with you. And I think it's special that you have the capacity to let people mean that much to you.

I know you'll meet different people, babi. Some will become your closest friends, some will teach you things about yourself that you didn't know before, and some will come into your life unexpectedly and end up meaning more to you than you ever imagined. But I also know that not everyone will stay. Some friendships might grow distant, some people might become strangers again, and some connections you thought would last forever might eventually become memories. I know that can be a difficult thing to accept, especially when you've genuinely grown attached to someone.

But I don't want the possibility of losing people make you afraid of getting close to them. You don't have to hold back from making memories just because you can't guarantee how long someone will be around. If someone makes you laugh, teaches you something, helps you through a difficult time, or simply makes a certain part of your life happier, then that connection has already given you something worth keeping. Not every person has to stay forever to have mattered. Sometimes, the value of knowing someone isn't measured by how long they're in your life, but by what you got to experience becauese they were in it.

And if you ever find yourself missing people who are not longer as close to you, I hope you can look back without feeling that you've lost everything they meant to you. It's okaky to miss how things used to be. It's okay to wish some people had stayed longer. You can acknowledge that you would've wanted a different ending while still being thankful for the memories you got to make. Growing up means learning that some things can be beautiful even when they don't last, and that people can remain a meaningful part of your story without being part of your everyday life anymore.

Babi, I want you to enjoy the people you have around you now. Don't let the thought that things might change someday keep you from appreciating what's happening today. Spend time with your friends, make memories with your family, and allow yourself to be present with the people who make this chapter of your life what it is. Someday, even the ordinary moments you're living now might become the ones you look back on most fondly.

Happy birthday, baba! I know you'll keep meeting people and building connections as life takes you through different places and experiences. I hope you never lose your ability to value those connections, even when they don't turn out the way you expected. And when you look back on your life years from now, I hope you'll have so many people to remember fondly, so many moments that still make you smile, and the comfort of knowing that even the people who didn't stay forever were still part of a life you got to live and love fully.
